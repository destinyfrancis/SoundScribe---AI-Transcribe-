SoundScribe · 聽得明

Privacy Policy · 隱私政策

✓ No data collected · 不收集任何資料

Overview · 概覽

SoundScribe (聽得明 – 語音辨認) is a macOS app that transcribes audio files and live dictation entirely on your device using on-device AI models. Your audio and transcripts never leave your Mac unless you explicitly choose to use an optional third-party AI service.

SoundScribe（聽得明）是一款 macOS 語音轉文字應用程式，使用本機 AI 模型在您的裝置上處理所有音訊。除非您主動選擇使用第三方 AI 服務，否則您的音訊和轉錄內容不會離開您的 Mac。

Data We Do NOT Collect · 不收集的資料

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your audio recordings.
  • We do not collect transcription results or text content.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect personal information such as name, email, or location.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or fingerprinting.

Microphone Access · 麥克風存取

SoundScribe requests microphone permission solely to enable the Live Dictation feature. Audio captured from the microphone is processed locally on your device in real time and is never saved or transmitted without your action.

File Access · 檔案存取

SoundScribe accesses audio and video files that you explicitly select or drag into the app. Output transcription text files are saved to the location you choose. The app uses macOS Security-Scoped Bookmarks to maintain access to your chosen folders across sessions; these bookmarks are stored locally in the app sandbox and are never transmitted.

Optional AI Services · 可選的 AI 服務

SoundScribe optionally integrates with third-party AI services (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic) for post-processing transcription text. These features are:

  • Off by default — you must explicitly enable them in Settings.
  • Bring Your Own Key — you supply your own API key; the app does not provide or proxy any API access.
  • Text only — only the transcribed text (not audio) is sent to the AI service.

When you use these features, your transcription text is sent directly to the respective provider under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy

API Keys · API 金鑰

Any API keys you enter are stored exclusively in the macOS Keychain on your device with WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly accessibility. They are never transmitted to our servers (we have none) and are never included in crash reports or logs.

On-Device AI Models · 本機 AI 模型

Speech recognition models are downloaded from Hugging Face Hub and cached locally on your device at ~/Library/Caches/. No audio or text is sent to Hugging Face during transcription — the models run entirely on-device using Apple Silicon.

Children's Privacy · 兒童隱私

SoundScribe does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect any personal data from any user regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy · 政策更新

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date below. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
